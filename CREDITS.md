# Credits

## Embedded recordings (2000s era instruments and the "Live 2000s" drum kit)
All embedded samples are released into the public domain under **CC0 1.0**:

- **Versilian Community Sample Library (VCSL)** — https://github.com/sgossner/VCSL
  (grand piano, harp, glockenspiel, vibraphone, hand chimes, kalimba, Dan Tranh zither, harpsichord,
  recorder, ocarina, drums and cymbals)
- **Versilian Studios Chamber Orchestra 2 Community Edition (VSCO-2-CE)** — https://github.com/sgossner/VSCO-2-CE
  (violin / viola / cello sections, flute, clarinet, oboe)

Samples were trimmed, looped, resampled and mu-law encoded by `tools/build_samples.py`.
Thank you to Versilian Studios for sharing these libraries.
